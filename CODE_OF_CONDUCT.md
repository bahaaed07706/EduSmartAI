# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people. This is a learning
project, and questions are welcome — there are no stupid ones.

## Expected behaviour

- Give and take feedback graciously. A review comment is about the change, not
  the author.
- Be patient with people who are new to the codebase or to the stack.
- Keep discussion on topic and in good faith.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, and any other
project communication — and to public spaces when an individual is representing
the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the repository author via a direct message or
a private channel. Reports will be reviewed and handled discreetly. The
maintainer may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may block repeat offenders.

This code of conduct is adapted, in spirit, from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
